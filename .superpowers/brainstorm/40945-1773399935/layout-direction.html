Vision & Mission — Layout Direction

The current layout stacks Vision (left) then Mission (right) as plain text blocks. Which direction feels right for the redesign?

Our Mission

A. Side-by-Side Cards

Two equal glass-effect cards on dark background. Clean, balanced, modern. Adds visual structure without heavy decoration. Good for symmetry between Vision & Mission.

Our Vision
To be the world's most trusted and innovative EPC enterprise.
Our Mission
Engineering excellence through mindful enterprising.
10+
Years of Vision

B. Bento Grid

Asymmetric grid with Vision as a hero card spanning full width, Mission + accent stat below. Trendy, dynamic, editorial. Creates visual hierarchy — Vision gets prominence.

Our Vision
To be the world's most trusted and innovative EPC enterprise.
Delivering world-class infrastructure and technology solutions that power sustainable development.
Our Mission
Engineering excellence through mindful enterprising.
Combining cutting-edge technology, sustainable practices, and unwavering commitment to quality.

C. Full-Bleed Split

Edge-to-edge two-tone split — each half gets its own background shade. Bold, architectural, Bechtel-inspired. The division line itself becomes a design element. Strong visual statement.

Recommendation

  • Option B (Bento Grid) is the most modern and editorial approach
  • Creates clear visual hierarchy — Vision leads, Mission supports
  • The asymmetry prevents the section from feeling like a generic "two boxes" layout
  • Easily extends to include accent elements (stats, icons, decorative geometry)

Trade-offs

  • A: Safe and clean but can feel generic / template-like
  • B: Most distinctive but slightly more complex to build responsively
  • C: Strongest visual statement but harder to balance on mobile (stacks to full-width blocks)
