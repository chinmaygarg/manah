## 4. Manah Aerospace & Engineering (MAEPL)

**Tagline**: "Futuristic aviation and MRO services, delivered with responsibility."
**Description**: Advanced aerospace and engineering solutions that enable safer, smarter skies for those who fly.

### 4.1 Services

#### Service 1: Aircraft Maintenance (MRO)
- DGCA certified business jet maintenance services
- Covers light and midsize jets to advanced aircraft
- Vision to expand into servicing Boeing and Airbus platforms
- **Key Capabilities**:
  - Customized maintenance for business jets with uncompromised safety and compliance
  - Parts procurement support to ease operational burdens for owners
  - Strict regulatory compliance and quality control
  - Responsive communication and client support
  - Dedicated engineering team for prompt, reliable, aircraft-specific maintenance

#### Service 2: Aviation Training (CAR 147)
- Empowering the next generation of aviation professionals
- **Programs**:
  - State-of-the-Art Level III Maintenance Training for all Embraer and ATR series aircraft
  - Level I and II allied trainings for foundational and advanced skills
  - Courses on aviation regulations for workforce knowledge and compliance
  - Curriculum integrating latest industry trends and technologies
  - Professional and qualified instructors

#### Service 3: Aviation Consultancy
- Strategic guidance for aircraft acquisition, operations, and compliance
- **Offerings**:
  - Aircraft Acquisition & Asset Management
  - Fleet Analysis & Market Research
  - Regulatory Compliance Solutions (manuals and documentation)
  - Advisory Service for operational efficiency

### 4.2 Certifications
- **DGCA CAR 145** — Indian aviation maintenance approval
- **145 Capabilities** — Detailed capability list
- **San Marino CAR 145** — International maintenance approval
- **San Marino 145 Capabilities** — International capability list

### 4.3 MRO Operational Structure
- **MRO Services** branches into: General Aviation, Narrow Body, Wide Body
- **Locations (MRO)**: Hyderabad, Koppal, Ahmedabad, Bhubaneshwar
- **Expansion Locations**: Pune, Bangalore, Chennai, Kolkata

### 4.4 Org Structure (Aerospace)

  - MM (Line Maintenance), MM (Base Maintenance), Store Manager, QM, Planning Manager, GHD Manager, Supply Parts & Supply Chain, BD
  - Line Stations with AME's & Techs

  - Training Manager, Examiner, QM, Training Faculty

  - MM (Narrow Body), MM (Wide Body), Works Shop Manager, Store Manager, QM, Planning Manager, GHD, Procurement, BD
  - Specialized shops: NDT, Battery Charging, W&B, Avionics, Structural Repair

**Glossary**:

### 4.5 Current Operations

2. DGCA-certified maintenance with proven reliability
3. Strategic partnership with AAI for hangar space at Begumpet, Hyderabad
4. Collaboration with Jet Aviation Dubai for global standards and best practices

### 4.6 Future Plans (Aerospace)

## 5. Manah Dynamics Pvt Ltd

**Tagline**: "Innovations & Implementations"
**Certification**: ISO 9001:2015

As the strategic arm of Manah Holdings, Manah Dynamics delivers end-to-end infrastructure and technology solutions powered by skilled talent and seasoned leadership, with a dual focus on **Projects & Infrastructure** and **Technology & Manufacturing**.

### 5.1 Projects & Infrastructure Division

Executes high-value projects with proven expertise, reliability, and speed. Operating across diverse delivery models: DBFOOT, DBOOT, BOO, BOOT, and Investment Projects.

**Sectors Covered**:
| Sector | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Power Transmission & Distribution | EHV lines, substations, underground cables — up to 765 kV |
| Building & Roads | Civil infrastructure |
| Disaster Management & Soft Skills | Training and response |
| Telecom | Towers, BharatNet, fiber network |
| BESS/SCADA | Battery energy storage, automation |
| M&A | Mergers and acquisitions |
| Defence | Systems, communication networks, composite technologies |
| Irrigation & Water | Water treatment, irrigation projects |
| Mining | Mining operations |
| Oil & Gas | Downstream processing |
| Renewables | Solar, Wind, Hydel, Green Hydrogen |

**Leadership Deliveries (Track Record)**:
1. EHV Transmission Lines, Substations, and Underground Cables — up to 765 kV
2. Pumped Storage Hydropower (PSHP), Hydropower Projects, 100 MLD Water Treatment Plants, and Irrigation Projects
3. Solar Projects — Rooftop and Ground-Mounted Installations
4. Defence Systems, Communication Networks, and Composite Technologies

### 5.2 Technology & Manufacturing Division (EMS)

A comprehensive Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) provider with strong R&D, design, assembly, and packaging capabilities. Supports global OEMs and emerging technology markets with end-to-end, design-to-delivery solutions.

**Capabilities**:
| Function | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Design & Engineering | End-to-end product development, prototyping, and innovation support |
| Manufacturing | Advanced PCB assembly, box build, and full system integration |
| Testing & Quality Assurance | Rigorous compliance testing and reliability validation |
| Supply Chain Management | Strategic sourcing, procurement, and logistics optimization |
| After-Sales Services | Comprehensive repair, maintenance, and warranty management |

**EMS Plant**: 30,000 sq. ft. facility in Cherlapally, Hyderabad — handles end-to-end electronics manufacturing from prototyping and PCB assembly to testing and final product integration. Supports both in-house production and OEM partnerships.

**Products**:
- Smart Energy Meters
- Smart Water Meters
- EV Chargers
- Room Chargers
- Defense RF Systems

## 6. Manah Green Energy Pvt Ltd

**Tagline**: "Powering a Cleaner Future Through Green Hydrogen"

A wholly owned subsidiary of Manah Dynamics Pvt Ltd, driving innovation in green hydrogen production through advanced renewable technologies. Supports India's clean energy transition and Net Zero 2070 vision.

### Progress to Date

| Milestone | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **MoU & Agreement** | Signed with Government of Madhya Pradesh for a Hydrogen Production Plant at Bina, based on ARB (Artificial Photosynthesis) Technology |
| **Technology Partnership** | Strategic partnership with SoHHytec SA, Switzerland, for water-splitting Artificial Photosynthesis technology for green hydrogen production |
| **Investment & Scale** | Plans to invest Rs 5,000 crores to produce 18,000 MTPA of green hydrogen in four phases over 350 acres of land near BPCL Bina Refinery |
| **Project Progress** | Offtake agreements and land allotment activities currently in progress |
